About us

Care that begins with grace

Graceful Living at Home: that is what we hope every client and family experiences with Neema Home Care.

Our story

Neema Home Care was created around a simple belief: growing older at home should feel safe, comfortable and dignified, and families should never have to feel alone while they arrange care.

The name “Neema” means “Grace.” We chose it because grace describes the way we want people to feel: respected, understood and cared for. It also describes how we hope to work with families: with patience, honesty and warmth.

We serve households across the Pacific Northwest, and we take the time to learn what makes each client who they are before we ever begin care.

Mission and vision

Our Mission

To deliver exceptional, client-centered home care that honors every individual with grace, preserves their independence, and brings absolute peace of mind to their families.

Our Vision

To be the PNW’s most trusted partner in home care, recognized for elevating the standard of aging in place through compassionate service, cultural dignity, and uncompromised quality.

Our values

What we stand for

Grace

Kindness and patience in every interaction, especially on difficult days.

Dignity

Every person is treated with respect, privacy and honesty.

Independence

We help clients keep doing what matters to them, in their own way.

Cultural Respect

We honor each person’s heritage, language, faith and traditions.

Quality

We hold ourselves to high standards and keep improving how we serve.

Compassion

We care about our clients and their families as people, not tasks.
