Who we work with

How we support referral partners

One-to-one, non-medical, in-home care that carries out the plan you and the family have put in place.

Hospital social workers & discharge planners

In-home help for a safe move from hospital to home: daily living support, meals, mobility and a watchful eye during recovery.

Rehabilitation & skilled nursing teams

Continued support after a rehab or skilled nursing stay: personal care, mobility, meals, transportation and encouragement to keep progressing.

Case managers & care coordinators

Caregivers who follow the established care plan, with clear, timely updates when a client’s needs or condition change.

Assisted living & senior communities

Supplemental one-to-one care for residents whose needs go beyond the community’s usual services: personal care, mobility, reminders and companionship.

Physicians & medical practices

Non-medical support between appointments: medication reminders, daily living help, mobility and observations shared with the family.

Placement agencies & senior advisors

Flexible care before, during or after a move, short-term or ongoing, so clients are supported through every transition.

Why partner with Neema

Care your patients can count on

  • Person-centered plans built with the client, the family and your recommendations.
  • Flexible schedules: hourly visits, 24/7 care or live-in care.
  • Prompt response to referrals, with care started as quickly as each situation allows.
  • Clear communication with families and, with the client’s permission, with you.
  • Cultural respect for each client’s language, food, faith and traditions.
Making a referral

Simple for you, reassuring for families

  1. Contact us

    Call (206) 474-7034 or email info@neemahomecare.org with the client’s contact details and the support needed.

  2. We reach out

    We contact the client or family promptly to arrange a free care consultation.

  3. Care begins

    A matched caregiver starts the plan, and we keep everyone informed as needs change.

Please don’t send medical records or detailed health information by email. Call us and we’ll arrange a secure way to share what’s needed.
