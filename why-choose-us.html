FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I get started?

Call us at (206) 474-7034, email NEEMAHomeCare26@gmail.com, or use the form on our contact page. We will set up a free consultation to learn about your needs and answer your questions.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. The first conversation about your needs is free and comes with no obligation.

Are your caregivers screened?

Choosing the right people to enter your home matters deeply to us. Please contact us to ask about our caregiver screening and hiring process; we are happy to talk it through.

Can care be customized?

Yes. Every care plan is tailored to the client’s needs, routines, culture and preferences, and it can be adjusted as those needs change.

What areas do you serve?

We serve the Pacific Northwest. Because coverage can vary by location, please contact us with your address area and we will let you know if we can help.

How are families kept informed?

We believe families should never be left guessing. We will agree with you on how and how often you would like updates, and we welcome your questions at any time.

Do you provide medical care?

No. Neema Home Care provides non-medical home care and companionship. For medical needs, please speak with your loved one’s doctor or healthcare provider.

What does care cost, and can insurance pay for it?

Costs depend on the type and amount of care needed. Please contact us for details about pricing and payment options.
