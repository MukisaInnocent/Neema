About this service

Care that fits into the rhythm of home

Some families need a few hours of help. Others need someone there through the night, or all day and every day. Our 24/7 and live-in care is built for those families: a dependable caregiver who becomes part of the household routine and keeps your loved one safe, comfortable and never alone.

Consistency matters. Wherever possible we keep the same caregivers in the home, so your loved one gets to know them, trusts them, and care feels like family rather than a service.

Our support is non-medical. We help with daily living and keep a watchful, caring eye on how your loved one is doing, working alongside the doctors and family who guide their health.

Two ways to have care around the clock

24/7 care and live-in care

Both keep someone close at all times. The right fit depends on your loved one’s needs and your home.

24/7 care

Caregivers work in shifts, so a fresh, attentive person is always on duty, awake and ready to help at any hour. A good fit when someone needs active help overnight or frequent support throughout the day.

Live-in care

One caregiver lives in the home, with scheduled breaks and rest time, and is available through the day and night. A good fit when someone needs steady company and help, but not constant hands-on care.

Common questions

24/7 & live-in care FAQ

Are your services available 24/7?

Yes. We can arrange care around the clock, including overnight, weekends and holidays. Call us and we’ll talk through the hours you need covered.

What’s the difference between 24/7 care and live-in care?

With 24/7 care, caregivers work in shifts so someone is always awake and on duty. With live-in care, one caregiver lives in the home with scheduled breaks and rest, and is available day and night. We’ll help you decide which suits your loved one.

Will my loved one have the same caregiver?

We aim for consistency so your loved one builds trust with familiar faces. If a caregiver is away, we arrange a replacement and keep your family informed.

Does the caregiver need their own room?

For live-in care, yes. The caregiver needs a private place to rest and sleep. If that isn’t possible in your home, 24/7 shift care may be a better fit.

Can we change the schedule later?

Absolutely. Care needs change, and we adjust the plan with you, whether that means more hours, fewer hours or a different arrangement.

Is this medical care?

No. Our care is non-medical: help with daily living, safety and companionship. We can remind clients about medications their doctor prescribed, but we do not provide nursing or medical treatment.
