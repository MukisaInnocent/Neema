Privacy Policy

A plain-language summary of how we handle your information.

Draft

This policy is a draft and should be reviewed by a qualified attorney before it is relied upon. Effective date: [to be added].

Who we are

This website is operated by Neema Home Care, serving the Pacific Northwest. You can reach us at NEEMAHomeCare26@gmail.com or (206) 474-7034.

Information we collect

We collect only what you choose to share with us, such as your name, phone number, email address, who needs care, the type of care you are interested in, your preferred contact method and any message you write, when you use our contact form, email us or call us.

Please do not send sensitive medical or financial details through the website form or by email.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your inquiry, discuss care options and follow up with you. We do not sell your personal information.

How the contact form works

The contact form currently prepares an email in your own email app, addressed to us. Your message is not sent until you send that email. The message is then handled by your email provider and ours.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser may share technical information, such as your IP address, with Google. We do not currently use advertising or analytics tools on this site. If that changes, we will update this policy.

Sharing

We share personal information only when needed to respond to you, when you ask us to, or when the law requires it.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete information you have shared with us by contacting us using the details above.

Children

This website is intended for adults and is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page.

Contact
