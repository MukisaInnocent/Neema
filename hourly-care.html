About this service

Support that fits around life at home

Not everyone needs someone there all the time. Many people do well at home with a helping hand for part of the day: a morning visit to get bathed, dressed and fed, an afternoon of company and errands, or an evening visit to get settled for the night.

With hourly care, you choose when visits happen and how long they last. As needs change, the schedule can change too, from a few visits a week up to daily care, or a move to 24/7 or live-in care if that becomes the better fit.

Our care is non-medical. We help with daily living and keep a caring eye on how your loved one is doing, working alongside their doctors and family.

Who it helps

Hourly care is a good fit when…

Daily routines are harder

Help with bathing, dressing and meals at the times of day that matter most.

Coming home from hospital

Extra support for the first days and weeks back home, following the care team’s guidance.

Family caregivers need a break

Respite visits so you can rest, work or run errands knowing your loved one is in good hands.

Loneliness is creeping in

Regular companionship visits with conversation, outings and shared activities.

What’s included

During a visit, we can help with

Every visit follows a personal care plan, built around your loved one’s needs, preferences and routines.

Personal care & hygiene

Bathing, dressing, grooming and toileting, always with dignity.

Mobility & transfers

Safe help moving around, getting in and out of bed or a chair, and fall-risk awareness.

Meals & nutrition

Preparing meals that suit tastes, culture and dietary routines, and encouraging fluids.

Medication reminders

Gentle reminders to take the medications their own doctor prescribed.

Light housekeeping

Laundry, dishes, tidying and keeping walkways clear and safe.

Companionship

Conversation, games, walks and a kind, familiar face.

Transportation & errands

Rides to appointments, shopping and community outings.

Dementia & Alzheimer’s support

Calm, familiar routines and patient reassurance for clients living with memory loss.

Respite for families

Planned time off for family caregivers, for a few hours or on a regular schedule.

Common questions

Hourly care FAQ

How many hours of care can we book?

It depends on what your loved one needs. Some families start with a few visits a week; others want help every day. We’ll talk through visit lengths and options during your free consultation.

Can we change the schedule later?

Yes. Add hours, reduce them or change the days. Care plans are meant to change as needs do.

Can hourly care become 24/7 care?

Yes. If your loved one needs more support over time, we can move to round-the-clock or live-in care.

Will we see the same caregiver?

We aim for consistency so your loved one builds trust with familiar faces. If a caregiver is away, we arrange cover and keep you informed.

Is this medical care?

No. Our care is non-medical: help with daily living, safety and companionship. We do not provide nursing or medical treatment.

Which areas do you serve?

Seattle and communities across Greater Seattle. See our service areas, or call us if you don’t see your neighborhood.
