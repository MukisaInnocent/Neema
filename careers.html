Caregiving opportunities

Everyday moments that make a difference.

Our caregivers help people with daily activities so they can stay comfortable, engaged and independent. All of this work is non-medical.

Personal care

Help with bathing, grooming, dressing and daily routines.

Companionship

Conversation, shared activities and a warm, steady presence.

Meal preparation

Preparing nourishing meals that match each client’s tastes and routines.

Light housekeeping

Tidying, laundry and keeping the home safe and comfortable.

Medication reminders

Gentle reminders to take medications as prescribed by the client’s doctor.

Mobility support

Safe, steady help moving around the home and with transfers.

Transportation & errands

Rides to appointments, shopping and community outings.

Memory care support

Calm routines and reassuring companionship for clients living with memory loss.

Overnight & live-in

Overnight, awake-overnight and live-in assignments, depending on client needs.

Before you apply

Ready to care. Committed to doing it well.

To protect our clients and keep our standards high, applicants need to meet the qualifications and screening requirements for the roles they are considered for.

Exact qualifications depend on the role, the service arrangement and current Washington State requirements. Approved training pathways and qualifying credential exemptions may be considered.

Submit your application
  1. Washington State Home Care Aide certification or another qualifying credential, where required
  2. Relevant caregiving experience (preferred)
  3. Current food worker card, where applicable
  4. Required background check
  5. Tuberculosis (TB) screening documentation, where required
  6. Able to perform assigned caregiving duties safely
  7. Reliable transportation when an assignment needs it
  8. Authorized to work in the United States, as applicable
Our commitment to quality

The small things are the big things.

Families trust Neema Home Care with the comfort and well-being of the people they love. We take that seriously. Every caregiver who represents Neema is expected to keep professional standards and follow the requirements of each assignment.

On designated awake overnight assignments, caregivers must stay awake and attentive for the whole shift.

  • Arrive on time and prepared
  • Follow each client’s care plan
  • Keep professional boundaries
  • Protect client confidentiality

A little more clarity

Questions about joining Neema?

Do I need caregiving experience?

Experience is preferred, but it is not always required. If you are compassionate, reliable and ready to learn, we encourage you to apply. Tell us about any experience you have, including caring for a family member.

Do I need a Washington State HCA or CNA credential?

Many home care roles in Washington require Home Care Aide (HCA) certification, or another qualifying credential such as a CNA, within the time frame the state sets. If you are in training or not yet certified, you can still apply. Tell us where you are in the process and we will talk through your options.

What areas do you serve?

We serve clients across Greater Seattle, including Seattle, Bellevue, Kirkland, Redmond, Mercer Island, Shoreline, Edmonds, Renton and Kent, as well as nearby communities.

Can I choose my availability?

Yes. You tell us the days and shifts that work for you, and we do our best to match you with assignments that fit. The shifts available depend on client needs.

Are overnight opportunities available?

Yes. Overnight, awake-overnight and live-in assignments may be available, depending on client needs. On awake-overnight shifts, caregivers must stay awake and attentive for the whole shift.

Is this medical work?

No. Our services are non-medical: personal care, companionship, help around the home and reminders to take medications a doctor has prescribed. Caregivers do not provide nursing or medical treatment.

How will I know if I’ve been selected?

If your experience, location and availability match a current or upcoming need, a member of our team will contact you by phone or email to arrange next steps. We may not be able to reply to every application individually.
